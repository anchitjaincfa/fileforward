# FileForward plan

## Thesis
A calm first step between DIY software and opaque tax-relief sales funnels. The MVP is a private educational router and synthetic marketplace demonstration, not a tax service.

## Four-round Claude review outcome
1. Firms—not the platform—set prices. No lead, placement, savings, refund, or outcome fees.
2. No real firms, data, handoff, accounts, payments, or advice at launch.
3. Deterministic routing, source-grounded content, and mechanically testable privacy.
4. Invariant result paths, conservative unsure warnings, deployed-header checks, and honest stale-content fallbacks.

## Future model
A firm publishes a binding diagnostic fee and a nonbinding service band, then gives a binding quote after review. Consumers pay firms directly. FileForward may charge a flat subscription based on coverage/capacity, never ranking or volume.

## Production gates
- 5 of 30 interviewed CPA/EA firms sign LOIs and publish diagnostics/bands.
- 3 accept a flat, non-contingent subscription.
- Each runs 10 off-platform diagnostics; at least 80% of quotes stay inside bands.
- Counsel approves operating model and state fee/referral rules.
- Reviewed IRC §7216 consent spec, WISP, retention map, credential verification, incident plan, and external pen test.
- Future data is enum-only, minimal, and has a TTL per field.
- Kill or pivot to a free directory/embeddable widget if supply fails.
